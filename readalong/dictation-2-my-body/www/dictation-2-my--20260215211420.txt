This is my body. 


My head is on top. 


My eyes help me see and my ears help me hear. 


I have two hands and two feet.


Look at Ali. This is his body. 


His hair is short and his hands are strong. 


He can run with his legs.


Look at Nada. This is her body. 


Her hair is long and her eyes are big. 


She can clap with her hands.


Now look at your body. 


Your eyes, your ears, and your hands help you every day!
