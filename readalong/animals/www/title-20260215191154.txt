Animals live all around us. Some animals live on farms, some live in the forest, and others live in the sea. 


A cow lives on a farm. It eats grass and gives milk. 


A dog lives with people. It is friendly and helps humans.


Wild animals live in nature. 


A lion lives in the forest. It eats meat and sleeps during the day. 


An elephant is big and strong. It eats plants and drinks a lot of water.


Animals have homes and babies. 


A bird lives in a nest and lays eggs. 


A cat lives in a house and has kittens. 


Animals help people in many ways. They give food, help with work, and make people happy.
